Record mismatches as bounded Todo issues. A visually or physically incomplete interaction remains open even when its core/API works.

Keep each In Progress issue finishable. If the discovered remainder exceeds the active increment, create linked Todo children before closing the accepted slice. Do not dispatch paid CI, push a build-triggering tag or claim release qualification from compilation alone.
