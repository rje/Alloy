# SRF-012: exterior rail surface Loft candidate

This bounded Loft mode retains one solved open arc, an ordered connected pair of solved lower lines, and one solved fit-spline rail. The rail must meet one free endpoint of each section. It is passed to OCCT as a spine with the authored sections placed at those exact endpoint contacts. The result is a valid open, zero-solid two-face sheet in a new component. The existing centerline surface and solid Loft modes remain available. Broader interior-contact, overrun, multi-rail, and handle-bridge Loft remain outside this ingredient. Public source observations and numeric uncertainties are recorded outside the repository at `../Alloy-reference-records/knowledge/tutorials/surface-iron-2026-10-14/source-contract.md`.

The builder identifies each face by its unique lower source-line span and the corresponding arc span, rather than output enumeration. Its free rail boundary and all seven edges receive retained source-derived IDs. The kernel gate checks both source sections and the rail against output boundaries with 41-point samples in each direction at a 0.01 mm threshold; this is **sampled numerical correspondence**, not proof of whole-curve identity. The prepared independent fixture uses an 80 mm separation, a 50 mm open arc, two lower lines, and a bowed endpoint-contact rail. Those numeric dimensions are Alloy fixture choices, not documented source values. It deliberately authors the first lower line in reverse orientation; oriented edge vertices, rather than geometric curve parameter order, determine chain traversal.

The core tests `surface_loft` and `surface_rail_loft` pass 2/2. The latter checks the two-face/seven-edge/zero-solid result, New Component ownership, stable IDs under a meaningful rail bend, history and reopen, and atomic broken-contact/missing-source refusal. The 37-call real-app API ledger, result, and inspected preview/commit/reopen captures are in `../Alloy-reference-records/deliverables/surface-iron-guide-loft-2026-10-14/api-01/`. It previews and cancels without authoring, commits through the shared Loft command, bends the rail and preserves face IDs, then undoes, redoes, saves, reopens and edits the Loft dialog. The independently authored prepared source and BRep are under the adjacent `core-01/` directory.

The Qt-delivered native check in `../Alloy-reference-records/deliverables/surface-iron-guide-loft-2026-10-14/native-04/` selects Rails in the actual dock, clicks the back arc, two lower lines and rail in the viewport, previews the two-face sheet and clicks Cancel. Its captured dock is readable, including selected counts, and the source remains unchanged. `recorded-console-output.txt` is copied verbatim from the completed Qt test tool result; the test was not rerun to make a log. The test does not claim OS-level pointer input. Surface Trim/Boundary Fill consumption and exact full iron geometry remain cumulative gates.
