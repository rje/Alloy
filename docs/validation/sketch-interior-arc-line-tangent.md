# Interior arc-line Tangent — SKT-117

Accepted 2026-10-18 for one authored circular arc and one finite straight segment with contact strictly inside both entities. The existing shared-endpoint Tangent case remains supported. The relation retains source IDs, signed line side, initial normalized contact parameters and sweep direction. On recompute, the solver preserves that contact branch and refuses an endpoint/out-of-segment or reversed-sweep result instead of rematching another branch. The exact choice of branch from current solved geometry is an Alloy policy; the public references do not specify click-location rules.

The real Alloy fixture changed arc radius 10→12 mm and drove the tangent line from y=11 to y=14 mm. Contact stayed inside the same arc sweep and finite line; an out-of-segment edit refused atomically. An arc and chord formed one closed rounded region, which extruded 5 mm to 1130.9733552923256 mm³ after the edit. The separate tangent segment touched the arc interior and was excluded from that profile. This tests downstream arc recompute without claiming that the two interior-touching entities form a single simple closed wire. Undo/Redo and save/reopen passed.

Focused solver checks cover both entity orders, sweep correspondence, duplicate relation and segment-boundary refusal. Native Qt input passed four test slots, including the existing endpoint case, both interior pick orders and selectable/deletable badge. Inspected captures show readable dimensions and the contact badge on an Apple M4 hardware-accelerated OCCT OpenGL 4.1 Metal viewport. Physical OS input, broader curve pairings and exact reference styling remain open.
