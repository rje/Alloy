# Pointer Line contacts with circles — SKT-085

Alloy now supports the bounded two-circle Line sequence from Step 3 of the external base-sketch public source contract. A Line started on an analytic circle retains its first endpoint on that circle. Holding Shift at the next click projects the free endpoint along that circle's tangent and retains a contact-point Tangent relation. A later ordinary click near a unique tangent contact on another analytic circle retains its ending endpoint on that circle and a second Tangent relation. The existing explicit circle-line Tangent route and its retained records remain unchanged.

The shared `sketch.pointerMove` and `sketch.placePoint` commands use the same circle-contact geometry. The input policy chooses a candidate within 10 logical pixels and refuses near-equal candidates within 0.5 pixel. Circle contact is preferred after existing exact point and origin snaps. An ambiguous tangent branch is refused; there is no automatic replacement of a selected point snap. Shift is required only for the from-circle tangent, matching the documented direction distinction. The exact reference hover tolerance is unknown, so these numbers are Alloy choices.

The contact-point Tangent record keeps the authored circle and Line IDs, the actual Line endpoint ID and signed side. Evaluation verifies that a matching `pointOnCurve` relation survives, the endpoint lies on the circle, the Line direction is perpendicular to the radius, and the contact remains on the finite Line. Deleting its required contact relation causes replay to refuse. A focused 10→11 mm circle-radius edit retains the relation. Ordinary older Tangent records still use their original solver primitive and finite-contact policy.
