# SKT-067: retained open-chain Offset

The sketch One Side Offset command accepts five ordered connected straight lines: two top shoulders, two slants, and a lower segment. Its signed distance is measured to the right of the ordered direction. The source lines remain authored or retained Project references; the five generated lines and their six endpoints keep stable IDs. Adjacent lines meet at OCCT line intersections. A collapsed or crossing result is rejected without changing the sketch.

On the cut-front fixture, the original top is the horizontal support through the two projected shoulder/slant junctions. It is not a fabricated central edge. The generated slants cross that support, and ordinary Line snaps to two retained intersection points to close the clearance profile at the original height. Those points and the Line follow source or distance edits. `sketch.openChainOffsetUpdate` edits numeric distance or a direct positive user-length name; the group stores the parameter UUID and signed side independently of the name. No expression parser or general curve offset is included.

The native Chain Selection policy expands the unambiguous lower horizontal/slant run, stops at the top shoulders, and lets each shoulder be added deliberately. API callers can supply the five ordered source IDs explicitly. Preview and Cancel do not author; OK publishes one sketch edit. The command is available through the same document/sketch authority from Qt and the local control API.

Focused core validation is `open_chain_offset`. The real-app runner `tests/open_chain_offset_api.py` uses the actual cut-front source fixture and checks Project of eight boundary edges, five-edge inward Offset, separate Line closure and selectable profile, direct Clearance binding, value/rename changes, undo/redo, save/reopen, and captures. Focused Qt widget validation is `openChainOffsetTypedClearance`, which types the direct name and clicks OK against the real sketch authority. The latest evidence and independent review are outside the repository under `../Alloy-reference-records/deliverables/open-chain-offset-2026-10-13/` and `../Alloy-reference-records/knowledge/tutorials/advanced-audit-2026-10-13/`.

This verifies the bounded Offset ingredient. It does not assert that the complete lid workflow is accepted or that the source's exact branch-resolution clicks were visible in public evidence.
