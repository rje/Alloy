# As-built Slider candidate

Status: isolated ASM-005 candidate independently accepted for this bounded ingredient on 2026-10-13; integration remains pending. The public workflow contract, citations, architecture and independent final review are held outside the repository in `../Alloy-reference-records/knowledge/tutorials/advanced-audit-2026-10-13/slider-design-review.md`, `asm005-design-proposal.md` and `asm005-independent-review.md` beside it.

One root-sibling Slider joint relates two distinct identity component occurrences. Component 1 moves and Component 2 defines the fixed assembly gauge. A straight retained edge on Component 2 supplies the Position; Alloy uses its midpoint as the joint origin. The demonstrated Y axis defines one relative translational coordinate with optional minimum and maximum limits. This slice supports one joint total, explicitly rejecting joint graphs or shared-occurrence relations. The exact public Position snap subtype and default limit/coordinate values were not established. The initial coordinate is zero; animation requires distinct enabled finite limits.

The joint record contains stable occurrence, body and edge IDs, name, axis, limit enable flags and values, and the committed coordinate. Driving the coordinate creates an undoable authored transaction and persists through save/reopen. Preview Motion and numeric preview use a transient coordinate; Stop/Cancel restores the committed coordinate without a history entry. The Browser Joints row exposes Edit and Animate Joint, and the ribbon Assemble menu opens the native joint controls. Escape and Cancel dismiss the native session. The selected ledge is highlighted; an orange midpoint and green positive-Y cue show the retained joint origin and axis.

Definition sketches, source references and BReps remain in rest coordinates. A joint-only edit validates against the existing evaluation and reuses its exact definition BReps. The viewport renders world-located body shapes, support faces and retained edges; it hides definition sketches during a nonzero assembly pose so they cannot appear detached. World bounds, face/edge picking and projection, Home fit, and component STL or single-body STEP export use the displayed pose. New modeling commands are rejected while the committed or transient coordinate is nonzero; user-length edits recompute definitions in rest context and clear transient motion. Source feature edits require returning the joint to zero first.
