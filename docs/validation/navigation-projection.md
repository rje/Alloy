# NAV-006: camera projection modes

Alloy now exposes **Orthographic**, **Perspective**, and **Perspective with Ortho Faces** in a visible Camera menu and the shared `view.projection` command. The current policy and resolved OCCT projection appear in viewport state; GPU diagnostics independently report the installed camera projection. Hybrid mode resolves orthographic within 0.5° of any of the six principal face directions, even after a custom/rolled camera pose, and perspective elsewhere. Switching modes keeps the current target, direction, pan and selected geometry. The fixed 45° perspective field of view and face-angle threshold are Alloy policies; public help documents the three modes and the hybrid face rule but does not provide those values.

The focused production-theme Qt fixture creates an asymmetric 40 × 27 × 19 mm box through the shared API. It opens the actual Qt Camera popup programmatically, passively verifies that it is the active popup and the Perspective row owns its screen pixel, then clicks that row. The result has the expected policy and actual GPU perspective camera, maintains the target and projects an off-center top-face point within 50 logical pixels of its former location; `selection.facePick` resolves the same retained top face. API calls then exercise hybrid resolution on all six named faces, isometric and small free-orbit poses on opposite sides of the 0.5° boundary. A second tab starts in Orthographic, and returning restores the first tab's Hybrid policy and resolved Perspective pose. Orthographic mode remains orthographic in isometric orientation. Authored state and undo remain unchanged.

The [external qt03 receipt](../../../Alloy-reference-records/deliverables/nav006-2026-10-14/qt03/) contains the exact CTest log (1/1, Qt 3/3), readable actual GPU captures of the Camera menu and three camera states, and the saved box document. The menu popup opener and model setup are Qt/API fixture actions; the menu-item click is actual Qt pointer input. Physical desktop input, other host/GPU combinations and complete DPI coverage remain separate navigation qualification. The camera policy is retained per open tab; the saved model is an authored-geometry artifact, not evidence that an ordinary camera pose is serialized into the document. Saved/custom named camera records are NAV-007 scope. No public runtime was used.
