# NAV-015: Fit Selection and planar Look At

The navigation bar now has visible **Fit Selection** and **Look At** controls. Fit Selection frames one currently selected visible body, face or finished sketch without rotating the camera. Look At aligns to a selected planar face or selected sketch plane; an active sketch delegates to the existing sketch Look At path. Both use the same local commands as their buttons and preserve the selected identity, authored document, undo state and stored custom orbit pivot. The selected shape is resolved from the current displayed world placement, with an exact occurrence required if a body has multiple instances. A finished sketch is framed from the normal, construction and centerline strokes already displayed by the viewport; hidden automatic support projections are excluded. Nonplanar, stale, ambiguous or absent selections return structured errors. Finished sketches hidden by an active assembly pose and staged body previews refuse rather than framing a definition/rest shape that is not displayed.

The public navigation reference documents Look At for selected faces, edges or lines and a separate sketch-palette Look At. This child admits planar faces and sketches; edge/line and nonplanar alignment remain open in the parent navigation scope. “Fit Selection,” the selected-shape scale margin, and camera target are Alloy policies because a separate named selected-frame control and precise framing values were not established by the public source. The custom orbit point is retained, while these commands target the fitted selected shape.
